Two-Tone v1.1

Controls

  • ← → or A D — move
  • Double-tap ← or → — dash
  • Space — cycle colour
  • 1–4 — pick a colour directly
  • N — use neutral (once banked)
  • M — mute/unmute
  • Drag, or tap the top-right dot — move / swap colour

Features

  • Match your colour to survive; a mismatch ends the round
  • More colours join the fall the longer you last
  • A white-ringed circle keeps shifting colour — risky, but worth more
  • Matches charge a shield that blocks one bad hit
  • Score enough to bank Neutral: a few seconds where every colour is safe
